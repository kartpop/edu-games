# Dino Walk — session design (draft for mark-up)

One game, one slow walk, two stops: **a / an**, then **numbers**. About 5 minutes. Words (2- and
3-letter) become a third stop later. The existing numbers game stays as it is, for full practice.

Built in `index.html` next to this file. Edit this file freely: strike lines, change words, add notes.

## Holding to the house rules

- Pastel colours, slow fades, quiet music box (same tunes as the numbers game).
- No points, stars, streaks, collecting or unlocking. Nothing is earned.
- No red and no buzzer. A wrong tap leads to "Let's look together".
- No replay button at the end. Parents restart from settings.
- Nothing seen or heard assumes a gender: "the dinosaur", "you".

## The screen

- **Path strip** along the top: a long-necked dinosaur walks left to right, past two signposts
  (one per stop), to a nest at the far end. It replaces the turtle as the "how far along" picture.
- **Main area** below: whatever the current stop needs (picture and cards, or the number board).
- **Top corners**, as now: ⚙︎ (long press, parents), music on/off, "say it again".

## The session, step by step

| # | What happens | Spoken | New clip? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start screen: the dinosaur and a Play button | — | — |
| 2 | Dinosaur walks to the first signpost (about 4 s) | "Let's go for a walk with the dinosaur." | yes |
| 3 | **Stop 1: a / an**, 6 things | "Look what the dinosaur found." | yes |
| 4 | Dinosaur walks to the second signpost | "Well done! Let's walk on." | "Let's walk on" |
| 5 | **Stop 2: numbers**, 4–5 questions | "Now, some questions." | no |
| 6 | Before the final question | "Last one!" | no |
| 7 | Dinosaur walks to the nest and falls asleep; a friend is waiting there | "All done! See you tomorrow. Bye bye from the dolphin!" | no |

The friend at the nest is the numbers game's random animal (36 of them, a different one each day,
all already recorded). It simply turns up; it isn't a reward.

There is no clock. The session ends when both stops are done, and the dinosaur's position follows
the questions answered, not the time.

## Stop 1: a / an

### One item

1. A large picture (emoji for now) fades in, with the word under it: **apple**.
2. Voice: "Apple. | Is it a, or an?"
3. Two big cards, always in the same places: **a** on the left, **an** on the right.
4. **Right tap:** the card and picture join into the phrase "an apple" on screen. Soft chime.
   Voice: "Yes! | An apple." Then the picture drifts down to the dinosaur.
5. **Wrong tap:** the card dims (no red).
   - Voice: "Let's listen together."
   - The first letter of the word is softly highlighted.
   - Voice: "Apple starts with A. A is a vowel. So we say, an apple."
     (For a consonant: "Tree starts with T. So we say, a tree.")
   - The right card glows; the child taps it. Voice: "Yes, that's it. | An apple."
   - That item comes back once, at the end of the stop.

### Choices I made (change any)

- **The voice never says the wrong form.** It asks "Is it a, or an?", not "a apple or an apple?",
  so the child only ever hears correct phrases.
- **The explanation names the letter, not the sound.** The child already knows the vowels, and
  the recorded voice is unreliable at bare sounds like "aaa". This is why the word list sticks to
  words where letter and sound agree.
- **Half and half.** Each session has 3 "an" things and 3 "a" things, shuffled, so always tapping
  "an" is wrong as often as it's right.
- **Every word once before any repeats**, remembered across days (as the animals are now).
- **A short recap closes the stop.** The six phrases appear in a row and are read once: "An apple.
  A tree. An egg…" It costs no new clips and is six more correct phrases heard.

## Stop 2: numbers

The same board, bead frame, questions, hints and spoken lines as the numbers game, so every line
is already recorded.

- **Five families:** bigger/smaller · before or after · ten more/less · missing number · beads
  (make a number, or tens-and-ones).
- **Each session:** 4 or 5 families picked at random, one question from each, in random order.
  Within a family the variant is random too (before *or* after; make-32 *or* 3-tens-and-2-ones).
- **Left out:** the counting warm-up and "tap the number". They stay in the numbers game.
- **Wrong answer:** exactly as now ("Let's look together", the dotted arrow, the glowing tile).
  A missed question comes back once at the end of the stop.
- Number rules carry over unchanged: tricky column edges half the time, ten more/less on whole
  tens only.

## Settings (parents, long press on ⚙︎)

- a/an things per session: 4–8 (default 6)
- number questions per session: 3–5 (default "4 or 5")
- number range: 1–50 or 1–100
- music on/off, voice on/off

## New recordings

About 120 short clips, well under $1, with the usual listen-and-approve pass:

- per word (37): "Apple" · "An apple" · "Apple starts with A"
- fixed (about 10): "Is it a, or an?", "A is a vowel" (×5 vowels), "So we say", "Let's go for a
  walk with the dinosaur", "Look what the dinosaur found", "Let's walk on"

Everything in the numbers stop and the ending reuses clips that already exist.

## Art

Emoji and a simple drawn dinosaur to start. The Blender dinosaur replaces the drawn one when it's
ready: three stills are enough (standing, mid-step, asleep).

## Decided

- The dinosaur has no name: it stays "the dinosaur".
- The written word shows under the picture.
- a/an always comes first, numbers second.
